# Key Fields

## Username
Your login name. This appears in audit logs and identifies your actions throughout the system.

## Full Name
Your display name, if configured.

## Role
Your assigned role (e.g., *User*, *Editor*, *Admin*).  
Roles determine which permissions you can receive.

## Permissions
A list of all permissions granted to your account.  
These control what you can do in the app, such as:

- Reading or editing Sales Orders  
- Reading or editing Works Orders  
- Accessing admin‑level features  

## Recent Activity
Shows your latest actions, including:

- Editing a Works Order  
- Finishing a Works Order  
- Creating or updating a Sales Order  
- Editing a breakdown  
- Logging in  

This helps you confirm whether an action was saved or logged.

---

# Actions You Can Perform

## View Profile Information
All users can view their own profile details.

## Review Permissions
See exactly what you’re allowed to do in the system.

## Check Recent Activity
Useful for confirming whether an action was completed or logged.

## Access Personal Settings
If enabled, you may be able to adjust display or notification preferences.

---

# Permissions Required
All users can access the My Profile page.  
No special permissions are required.

However:

- What you *see* elsewhere in the app depends on the permissions listed here.  
- If a button or field is disabled, check this page first.

---

# Workflow Context
The My Profile page sits outside the main workflow (Sales Orders → Works Orders → Breakdown → Finish).  
Its purpose is informational:

- It explains who you are in the system  
- It explains what you can do  
- It explains why certain actions are allowed or blocked  

---

# Notes & Tips

- If something is disabled elsewhere, check your permissions here.  
- If you need additional permissions, contact an administrator.  
- Your username appears in all audit logs — this page helps you verify your identity.  
- If you see unexpected activity in your recent actions, notify an admin.
